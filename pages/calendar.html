Fasting days & festivals

The Ekadashi, vrat and festival dates our kitchen follows, so you can order your grain-free meal a day ahead.

Where these dates come from. They are the published dates of the Vaishnava calendar our kitchen itself observes — we never work out a tithi ourselves. When two calendars we follow place the same observance a day apart, we show the one our temple follows and note the other beside it.

Ekadashi meals are cooked on request. Order online or call us a day ahead and it will be ready — grain-free, with sendha namak, on clean utensils.
