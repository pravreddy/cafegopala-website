Where these dates come from. Each colour is one published calendar our kitchen follows — the temple's own list, the Vaishnava calendar for Bangalore, and so on. We never work out a tithi ourselves. When two calendars place the same observance a day apart, we show the one our temple follows and note the other beside it.
